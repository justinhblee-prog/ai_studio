3D Studio

Preview garments and characters in 3D and place them in scenes.

Aimed at checking fit and texture you cannot judge from flat photos.

  1. Pick a 3D file from your library
  2. Change angle and lighting
  3. Save as angle images or a turntable video
In detail

When you use it

Uses

  • Inspect your own 3D files (GLB)
  • Save angle images and 360° turntable videos
  • Reuse saved images in Photo, Try-on and Video

What goes in and out

In: 3D file (GLB)

Out: Five angle images, a 360° video, eight turn frames for Video

How it differs

A service of its own

Usable on its own, separate from try-on.

Our engine, our library

Uploaded 3D files are kept in your library for the other studios.

Camera and lighting presets

Front, three-quarter, side, back; soft, window, golden-hour light.

Where it really stands

  • Works GLB upload, viewing, rotating and background change (test stage)
  • In development Photo-to-3D, saving angle images and rotation videos, and garment 3D / size-by-size fit analysis are in development (feasibility under study)
