Photo Studio

Upload product photos, make lighting drafts, and let your team review and approve.

Handle product images for shops and lookbooks in one place. Originals never change; edits stack up as versions.

  1. Upload product photos (with rights confirmation)
  2. Create brightness and lighting drafts
  3. Request review, then approve or ask for changes
  4. Export only approved results as a lookbook package
Functions

From one photo to a finished image

26 functions, connected in one workspace.

  1. 1

    Import

    • Upload with rights confirmationCannot upload without confirming rights.
    • Bulk upload (up to 20 photos)Each photo goes through the same rights check; failures are listed with a reason.
    • ZIP import and SKU groupingGrouped by SKU.
    • Link photos to products (SKU)Keep photos tied to catalog items.
  2. 2

    Background

    • Background removalKeep the product only (transparent PNG).
    • White, solid, gradient backgroundsMake standard shop shots at once.
    • Scene background replacementPlace the product in studio or outdoor scenes.
    • ShadowAdd a grounded shadow to a transparent-background image.
    • ReflectionAdd a floor reflection.
  3. 3

    Adjust

    • Brightness and lighting draftsOriginals untouched; results saved as new versions.
    • White balanceNeutralizes a color cast (adjustable strength). Matching to a color reference is in development.
    • Wrinkle, dust and blemish cleanupLogos, labels and text are preserved.
    • Product shape and logo checkFlags drift from the original before review.
  4. 4

    Size and format

    • Auto crop and canvas fitTrim to the product, then center it on a canvas without cropping or distorting.
    • Per-channel size presetsSave size, ratio and file rules per channel; values are checked against each channel before launch.
    • UpscalingEnlarge small originals cleanly.
  5. 5

    Styling and variants

    • Compose onto mannequin, model or sceneContinues in the same workspace as Virtual Try-on.
    • Colorway variantsOne photo, many colors.
    • Multiple shots per productGrouped angle and composition variants.
  6. 6

    Batch and presets

    • Batch processingApply one job to many photos.
    • Save brand presetsSave combos so the team keeps one look.
  7. 7

    Compare, review, export

    • Version historyEvery edit stacks as a version.
    • Review → approve, request changes, rejectChanges and rejections need a comment.
    • Export approved results as a lookbook packageEditors and above only.

In detail

When you use it

Uses

  • Consistent white-background, spec-ready shop shots
  • Many photos of one product with the same adjustments
  • Download only after team review and approval

What goes in and out

In: Photos (JPG, PNG), SKU

Out: Versions, lookbook package (ZIP), channel files

How it differs

Originals never change

Every edit is a new version you can roll back.

Review is built in

Request, approve, change or reject; only approved work is exported.

Rights are recorded

You must confirm rights before uploading.

Where it really stands

  • Works Adjust, versions, review, export
  • In development Background removal, batch and channel export are in development

FAQ

Are my photos used for AI training?

Off by default; you must turn it on yourself.

Is the original overwritten?

No. Always saved as a new version.
