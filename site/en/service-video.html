In detail

When you use it

Uses

  • Short Reels, Shorts or feed videos from approved images
  • Join 3D turn frames and try-on results
  • Aspect, motion, transitions and captions in one place

What goes in and out

In: Images (library or uploads)

Out: Video file (WebM), a spec sheet

How it differs

The library is the source

Pull in uploads, 3D turns and past results.

Set for where it will be posted

Pick 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9.

Ask in plain words

Say "slow zoom, soft transition".

Where it really stands

  • Works Joining images into a downloadable video (in browser)
  • In development AI image-to-video follows the provider connection

FAQ

How long can a video be?

One to six seconds per image, joined in order.
